you know what?  
sometimes I’m sick of you  
I want to scream in your face  
tell you everything you do is unfair  
and I’m such a better person than you   
I want you to understand everything I do  
and make it clear I know you’d never know  
or understand all that I carry and all my woes  
that you’re inexperienced and naive and clueless  
you piss me off, you so totally piss me off with your  
lack of communication, making me feel more involved  
making me feel crazy for saying what you asked me for  
I really hate the way you want me to be different than them  
when all I’ve ever been is them; all I can ever be is simply me  
who wants to be called pretty, who wants to be enough to marry   
who wants to be wanted the way everyone wants someone feminine  
who wants to be warm enough and strong enough to be seen as a mom  
who wants to act crazy and by my attractiveness have the behavior excused  
the way an in-love husband seems to always do whenever his wife is hormonal  
I want to have the stereotypes no matter how much that’ll make you despise me
