ever deal with a man with a work wife?  
not a single sight more embarrassing, I guarantee it  
not only do you have to pat the back of one child  
the one who might give you another to bear if you can bare it  
you have to cringe at the whiny, infantile shrieks of someone born long before you  
who has the capacity to speak normally as long as it’s only women in her view  
who, like a vulture, feeds off the youth of others  
with talons, hanging off of him, desperate for one more after five embraces  
when she knows damn well everyone else, like normal people, only need one to leave  
she thinks interjecting with nonsense will give her something she’ll never have  
because she expects unsuspecting men to hand it to her  
as long as her voice is high enough, hair is bleached enough, maybe then she’ll see her self as enough  
but what stops her is the snickering of people behind her, the lions can smell desperation  
and she is dripping, oozing, leaving others to trudge through it, slowing each and every step  
of any other with the misfortune of crossing her path  
every circumstance becomes a joke  
a need to say something—anything—to get a reaction  
even when there’s no tact, no substance  
she has not a single idea that isn’t regurgitation of what she sort of kind of heard   
so long as the original was birthed from facial hair  
you can tell by the way she squirms when making eye contact  
with other women  
if men aren’t the center of her experience—her logic, her gossip  
she’s like a drowning sea pup in the middle of an ocean  
she gives the quickest hug to anything with breasts  
and lingers on anyone she sees as having a mouth worthy of giving her validation   
do you think she is so scared  
scared to make women the center  
because looking at one—making eye contact  
will show that she has a witness?
