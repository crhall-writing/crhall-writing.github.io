---
title: tortured artist
date: 2026-08-10
layout: poem 
---
I remember months ago  
wishing for nothing but solitude  
and now that I have it  
I have no thought on what to do  

the possibilities are too endless  
I want to make Aidan those gifts  
that portrait and that story  
before the opening of his exhibit  

but all I feel  
is drained and hollow  
finally with comfort  
finally without sorrow  

and how is it that I  
who am always spouting about  
the absurdity the “tortured artist”  
a myth worth not its crowd  

now needs the same  
to be tortured beyond belief  
to ponder my pain  
to put paper—any type—to ink?  

is it possible to feel too much  
to bear existing  
yet not enough  
to create anything?
