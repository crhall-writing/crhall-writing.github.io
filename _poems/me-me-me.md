---
title: me, me, me
date: 2026-08-09
layout: poem
---
once upon a time  
I wrote and wrote and wrote  
wrote until the sun would rise  
and wrote until I’d cry  

what has happened?  
what has turned on its side?  
what has made me unable to think  
in the way a once prolific artist would?  

did I need him all along?  
someone to challenge me  
someone to make me feel young?  
naive?  

because now I feel so lived and so old  
I feel like I no longer will experience anything real  
I’ll be locked in a repetitive fog  
until my legs and arms shrivel up   

because not only do I need to put in forty hours  
find a normal job that pays my bills in a year or two  
I need to learn how to behave  
how to parent someone younger and new  

but am I able to let go of the spotlight?  
the concept of me, me, me?  
where everything I create  
is about me, despite what I’d want you to see  

I’ll miss being selfish  
I’ll miss being little miss self centered  
where the colors of the world underscore my greed  
where I can use my grief to pool my sympathies  

and have the rest look on them on my rainy days  
force the world to recognize how much I’ve experienced  
to moan on how little I’ve grown  
and even the admission is something I feed
