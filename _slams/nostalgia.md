date: 2026-09-11

---
I was listening to “back on 74” by jungle  
what a fucking track, right?  

but anyway, it got me thinking  
thinking about the term nostalgia  

am I the only one who doesn’t get nostalgia?  
sure i get “ferveh”, which is like, the opposite of homesickness  
it literally translates to far-sickness  

does anyone else get particularly sad when you have to celebrate a holiday?  
not knowing where to go  
because he will be there?  

not knowing where to go  
because you’re not welcome anywhere permanent?  

and whenever you’re welcome  
you know it’s because they want something from you  
something to take that you will never get back  
something you can only keep when you are alone  

does anyone else feel that?  

you gather up the small bits of courage you’ve ever had  
to say piece out, I’m going to… somewhere  
somewhere that feels equal in scale to a room full of people  
somewhere that can fill that hole that you only know is there  
by observing others around the holidays  

but something I came to terms with, as I’ve aged  
is that I don’t have nostalgia  
all I want is life to go on and on  
even through tragedy, even through labor, even through embarrassment  
because at least, now, I am no longer a kid  
staring with wide eyes for someone to protect me  
that luxury is not a guarantee  

and at least I can say  
I never peaked during childhood, high school  
yes, I did go on to live a much fuller, alive life  

and I wish I could package it all up nicely  
but packages remind me of christmas time  

and I don’t think I’ll ever fully get away  
from the sadness that comes around the holidays
