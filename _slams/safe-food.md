the only safe food to eat in america right now   
is a spongebob popsicle from the local ice cream truck    
with the bubblegum eyeballs that you’re incapable of absorbing    
and I hope you know that I know that you know we don’t need to get into the details of that    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
is the chip on your shoulder    
if you’re lucky it’ll be sour cream and onion flavored    
but I tend to have mine coated in extra salt and vinegar    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
are all of the pills that are too big to swallow    
if you’re lucky they’ll give you a pill crusher    
and I’ve used many of those, I found they work so poorly    
that pounding them on the counter with a run of the mill hammer    
works so much better    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
is the kate farms organic sole-source formula    
and what really gets digested is whether or not the name is fair use in this poem    
and not that their ads still target me every now and then on social media    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
are the hundreds of milligrams of caffeine I consumed    
staying up with you all night in the hospital    
because the nurses didn’t have the training to put a tracheostomy tube back in    
if they accidentally knocked it out    

and I know I can’t blame the nurses outside of the ICU    
the floor requires four night nurses to handle the entire cardio unit each night    
and you didn’t even have a heart problem    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
are the words you’ve gotta keep inside    
just until the time is right and the hole in your throat can close back up    
you’ll re-learn to swallow first, then speak, then drink, then finally eat    
something besides those god-awful crates of kate farms sole source formula    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
is the head of your surgeon that I and your wife wish we could bite off    
the one who didn’t have the courage to face you    
after stealing your entire ability to speak or breathe or sleep or eat     
and leaving us without a clue on what to do    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
are the unmarked meds in hundreds of bottles sent over from Lloyds Pharmacy    
who, only if you’re lucky, will provide more than 3 doses when you were prescribed 5 a day    
and see putting proper names on the containers as a luxury reserved only for those who aren’t dying    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
are the hundreds of doses of narcotics they don’t let you flush down the toilet    
they are the ghosts staring coldly at you in your passenger’s seat    
shoved into a meager backpack    
as you drive from place to place, each one telling you to move onto the next    
the pharmacies will tell you to go to the police, and the police will tell you to go to the pharmacies    
and it’ll be hours you will spend, driving in your car    
the golden hour often brings a pain even on days you didn’t just watch someone fade into their death bed    
and oxy would never have sounded as fucking good as it does RIGHT NOW    
but eventually you find a disposal point, at a random pediatric center    
and you take over fifteen minutes putting your vessels in the bin, one by one    
in front of sniffling children, their parents, what an audience for my performance    

the only safe food to eat in america right now    
is whatever tastes good enough    
to get you to shut your mouth
