lately, I’ve been playing this thing called “the clown game”  
I got it from someone on instagram called “clown daddy speaks”  
please please please check them out  

but the way it works, if you want to play it too  
is to point at any object and call out what it definitely is not  
and it warms up your creativity  
for me I found it shows what my mind gets stuck on  

for example, this is not a zucchini  
and that is not bottle of wine, unfortunately  
and we are not in kansas  

and you are not a grand piano  
and they are not a thunderstorm  
and we here are not a tornado  

at least not in the literal sense, you know  

and I am not a brain surgeon  
and I am not a therapist  
and I am not a housewife from 1955, I’m sorry  

and Woody Allen movies are not good  
and explaining that to you proves our mismatch  
and that is not tungsten  
nor uranium-234 

and he is not the demon core  
he won’t emanate plumes of radiation that will kill you  
so why do I feel my cells actively undergoing destruction  
and my body pausing marrow production  
why do I feel that the skin of my jaw is going to fall off  
just as it happened to the other radium girls  

you are not his world  
and big coincidence, I was never either  
and that sinking feeling in both of our stomachs  
will never make him see anything clearer  

that is not the way friends act  
that is not the way coworkers act  
and this is not in fact the way love should feel  

and that is not heaven  
and that is not hell  
and he was never really an angel  

and this , this is not how being over a breakup feels  
but it is what progress looks like  

and you are not a loser, and he isn’t either  
and you are not a winner, and he isn’t either  
and none of this  
was ever  
really  
a game
