we’re not so different, you and I  
you sit on your high horse, and I sit on mine  

we both want something greater than this world can provide, huh?  

but we fucking want to strangle each other  

every time we have to look each other just past the eyes  
because eye contact is hard  
for people like you and I  

at one point you must’ve been 27, too  
trying to turn looping thoughts into some stairway to heaven  
something linear instead of periodic  
because it truly sucks every time we wrap around and end up just as we started, huh?  

if we’d both just learn to keep our heads down enough and thoughts severed enough  

from our bodies because propriety, mutual anguishing squeaky clean clarities mimicking sanity  
are what we’re here for—we are, aren’t we?  

we’re here for the truth, first and foremost  

but I think the reason you gave me any attention in the first place  

is that I could let you know all the alternate systems in which  

one wasn’t actually the first, so the first place could be ripped and torn until it was unrecognizable  

did you know sets exist where 4 is 0 and 5 is 1  

and god does that make a person gaslight-able, huh?  

when I’ll say over and over until my lungs give out  

that these principles don’t cater to your intuition  
it’s like you saw that quality in me as something that didn’t showed promise    
but instead ease of manipulability 

I will forever doubt my own gut instincts  
I’ve been taught that way to live my entire life  

but you’ve been exactly where I am, huh?  
I can see it in the way your brows furrow every moment they doubt you  

simply because you decided to wear your hair down one day  

I see it in the way you secretly take screenwriting classes at the local community college  
and requested an office without windows so the sun wouldn’t force you to observe the passage of time  

we’re not so different, you and I  
sure you got rejected from that promotion, when you asked why  
they said because of your anger issues, and oh my god, duh, you have anger issues, what a no-brainer  
but man is it a bummer that no one will ever see that being propositioned every time you attend a “professional conference”  
having those around you not protect you but instead tell you to change your attitude because a nice girl would find it all of this fun and flattering  
and oh my god, does that all change the very essence of you, imagine how you’d behave if the path of a man were laid before you  

you’d think scientists would’ve caught on by now  
but as has always been the case  
all the available data came from studies performed on men   
and that’s such a goddamn shame, huh?
