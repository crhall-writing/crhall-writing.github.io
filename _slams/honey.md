title: honey
date: 2026-09-14
layout: poem
---
aw, honey    
look at me  
tell me just one more time  
that this isn’t over  
that this isn’t the last I’d ever see of your nose  
slightly crooked  
and what scares me most is I don’t even remember now  
if that’s your nose I’m thinking about  

aw, honey  
let me call you just one more time  
by one of the thousand pet names we made for you  
let me call you the love of my life  
because that term itself became nothing more than a nickname, right?  

aw, honey  
don’t make me face reality  
we both know how overstimulated, how in need of recharge time, how sensory deprived  
I feel when the truth is forced down my windpipe  
how I choke on it until I vomit  
and make it your problem just as much as mine  

aw, honey  
don’t dare come to my places  
and see all the new faces  
I’ve been thinking on, it’s not the appearances that are better, I swear  
it’s that I can feel freer and lighter and smarter and kinder  
the more I spend time around anyone who isn’t associated with you  

aw, honey  
this is gone, aren’t we?  
secrets and inside jokes and nicknames would never make up  
for a lack of depth  
chemistry doesn’t imply longevity  
and richness doesn’t grant me survival
