title: this goes out 
date: 2026-09-20
layout: poem
---
this goes out to all the plans I waited on  
because I wanted to be loved  

if I could sit my pride down  
tell her I’m sorry  
for the wait on repairing her  
for the delayed time spent  
counting how many days until I’d again feel plain  
not special, not problematic, just normal and congruent    

I wish I could give roses to my skin  
as it formed bumps and craters from the stress  
of trying to fit into a pigeonhole that was never principled enough for me  

can I still write a card to all of my lost sparks  
the ones whose glimmers I’m starting to glimpse again 
or to the vomit and nerves  
like scribbles in my stomach, gnawing the lining  
or to the way I’d glue my eyelids together the moment I saw a notification shining on your phone  

is it better that I looked away as opposed to having a morbid curiosity?  
if every organ in my body shuts down, but I respected your privacy  
is that enough to keep something—even a ghost ship, its crew skeletal and dusty—afloat?  

no—duh  
of course it’s not  
and I’m a smart girl, I know right from wrong  

so this goes out to my beliefs, to my gut instincts, to my abilities to reason  
to every lesson I’ve ever learned from others  
and to the way I know I’ll be one day ready to abandon all these reflexes again  
the moment I really think I’ll hold them forever  

and now I don’t really know how to end this  
but I am notorious for keeping something going  
for far longer than I should
