I saw the best minds of my generation  
stuck  
suckling, teething, rocking back and forth  
imploring and whoring their minds and bodies and futures  
to a club that will never see credentials as enough  

I too have lost sleep  
steeped in frenzied dreams about how proud of me they'd be if I just found out the secret I needed to be  
enough  

from the outside it looks like a warm living room  
seen from the other side of a frostbit window  
they’ll never let you know  
exactly what they speak of  

for these words can only be heard  
by those who have done  
whatever it takes to be enough  

there are pats on the backs of pals  
comradaries formed from lucrative and sacred spells  
and I’m still postholing, alone in the snow  
hoping if I reach that post, reach that goal  
that I’ll finally, finally, finally be let in  
but they fail to mention, it shifts  
and if you listen, when you’ve finally gotten near it  
in the distance, you may hear whispers not of penitence,  
but of metaphysics and david foster wallace  
and you can read, you can solve  
telling yourself and your grandma that this is what it is to evolve  
and just when you think someone, one of them,  
has finally let you into the in, into the mixture,  
you can only take a few breaths before receiving a picture  
you can only mention that you love women too  
a single time before they ask which day or week or month they can watch—  

you, like Zeno, will never have the privilege of being told to stop  
movement exists for those who escape his paradox  
but that goal is too far, too far off for the wives of Kerouac and Carr   

you catch where I am—where we are?  
I’m with you in Rockland  
are you madder than I am?   

my father told me to always be the smartest in the room  
but these days I think  
those are the types I’d trust least with my drink  

and maybe  
whatever was butchered out the body  
good for 1,000 years’ eat  
will be  
whatever I let them take from me
