---
title: the circus 
date: 2026-09-13
layout: poem
---
he always designed the circus, never performed in it  

and you could tell his lack of makeup penetrated his surface  

just as hard as he would me  

he was never fully in any community  

and I felt sorry, aggrandizingly sorry  

for the way he never seemed to fit in  

and I saw myself in him  

me at 20, 22, 24, and 26  

and at 27 something split  

like an atom  




but when the lights were off  

when everyone else was in bed  

he’d corner the mirror  

just as he would me in the shower  

and god did I love the way he’d look down on me  

his reflection would be illuminated by incandescents  

black liner and white paint would cake his juvenile face    




and for a minute or two   

he could feel whole, consequential  

to the glorious problems around him   

he would become one of them     




but the train is leaving  

it’s taking the elephants and bears and the rest  

and now you’re left  

with nothing but emptiness and hope
