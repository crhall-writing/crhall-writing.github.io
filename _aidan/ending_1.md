I talked about this day coming  
as if I knew it would  
each day I somehow was capable of imagining  
what is now unimaginable  

I didn't think this level of pain was possible  
maybe I've felt it before  
surely I've felt worse  
but at the moment, it's a gut-twisting sore  

I suppose now I hear my heartbeat  
after it dropped with agonizing speed  
you were so ready to leave  

and I needed more time  
more words, more embraces, more chances  
for change that would never come  
I wanted to experience, suddenly, discomfort  

the same discomfort I punished you for bringing  
the same discomfort which inspires my writing  
I still don't know which is better  
being alone or being naked  

you told me you hoped I found the perfect person  
who would let me be my one and true self  
I can't believe it isn't you  
I needed it to be you  

but it isn't  
I know that now  
and I thank you for showing me  
because clearly I was never strong enough  

but I wish we had one more chance  
one more day to be in each others' arms  
one more day to climb mountains and camp  
one more day to kiss you as the sun comes up  

one more day to pretend we are happy  
one more day to ponder our future children  
one more day to be alive and well  
one more day to go on a long drive  

one more day to sing together  
I remember one night you encouraged me to sing  
telling me my voice was beautiful  

then when you were in the shower  
I could hear myself singing louder  
just to be heard, for you to compliment me  
but you only left the bathroom eventually  

and now after having words from my best friends  
laughing about how ridiculous I've been  
I don't think it was my fault  
but I'll be sad for a few months  

maybe more, who knows  
you never got to hear my piano  
or my guitar or my real singing voice  
or the drawings of you I was making  

I look over at our scrapbook  
the hardest one to go  
and I wonder if you're sitting out there  
beneath my porch  

I don't think you are  
I think you probably did the same  
calling all your friends  
getting the reasons for why you were right  

I did that as well  
and it's what one has to do  
to imagine the unimaginable  
does it feel as hard for you?
