title: chateau
date: 2026-07-14

---
there lies a chateau  
on the island off Alexandria Bay  
once promised to sweet Louise  
that was, ’til her heart gave way  

and the heart of George broke  
clean, dreams stringed into two  
just like my life had  
the day I’d lost you  

and I saw decades down the line  
frozen in that same shot of time  
I couldn’t put a dish in its place  
that had laid out that night  

you’re the inspiration of my life  
the sunrise and sunset all in one  
and I almost let you go  
I can’t believe we’ve another run  

another chance to hold you close  
today enveloped in you I cried   
and each tear was another reason  
that you’ve brought the happiest tide  

and maybe I’ll never match what you bring  
but I like to think I do  
you make it happen so naturally  
and you take such care, too
